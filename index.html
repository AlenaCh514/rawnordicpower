About

Welcome to Raw Nordic Power — your immersive journey into the heart of the North.

Music of strength, courage, and an unbreakable spirit.

Here, the raw intensity of Viking battle anthems meets the deep, atmospheric sounds of the North — war drums, thunder, and voices that refuse to fall.

Ragna leads our pack from the fjord: a fictional shieldmaiden, forged from the sagas. Her face and voice are created with AI; the music and the stories are made with love.
